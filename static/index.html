How access works
Managers can view all reports, manage members, create assignments, and access the full dashboard.
Candidates can log in, complete the consent screen, and conduct voice/chat interviews that generate process reports.
Due dates are mandatory. After the due date passes, the candidate cannot submit further reports unless the manager extends the deadline.
Roles are set when the account is created and can be changed at any time by a manager.
